Custom cabinets, built to your walls

Stock cabinets come in fixed widths, and the gaps get hidden with filler strips. Custom cabinets are drawn to your room — the out-of-square corner, the odd ceiling height, the window that isn't centered — so every inch works and the finished wall looks like it was always there. We measure in person, design the room in a photorealistic 3D rendering, price it honestly, and install it with our own team.

National Closet Company is a division of Blair Custom Interiors, our family's custom cabinetry and interior remodeling company. That is what lets a closet company build kitchens, vanities and built-ins to the same standard, with one team from the first measurement to the last hinge adjustment.

Custom cabinets for every room in the house

Most of our cabinet projects fall into one of these rooms. Each one starts with the same free in-home design visit.

  • Kitchen cabinets — full kitchens, replacement cabinet runs, islands with seating, pantry walls, coffee bars and butler's pantries.
  • Bathroom vanities — floating or furniture-style vanities, linen towers and medicine cabinets with fitted drawers.
  • Laundry rooms & mudrooms — upper cabinets, folding counters, hanging rods, lockers, benches and cubbies.
  • Built-ins — dining hutches, window seats, bookcases, fireplace surrounds and media walls that hide the television and the cables.
  • Home offices — built-in desks, file drawers and shelving walls, the same way we build office storage.
  • Garage & utility cabinets — tall cabinets, workbench bases and wall cabinets that pair with our garage storage systems.

The look: painted, wood-grain, and the details that make it custom

Shaker, flat-panel and inset door styles; painted colors or wood-grain finishes; brass, black, nickel or bronze hardware; soft-close doors and full-extension drawers throughout. Inside the boxes you choose the fittings: deep pot drawers, pull-out trash, spice pull-outs, tray dividers, drawer organizers and lighting. We bring samples to your design visit so you can hold them up in your own light.

How much do custom cabinets cost?

Custom cabinets are priced per project — by the linear footage, the door style, the finish and the fittings inside — so we don't quote them from a range the way we publish starting prices for closets and pantries. A painted laundry-room run and a full kitchen with an island are very different jobs. What you get instead is an exact, written price at your free in-home design, priced option by option so the decisions stay yours, and paid the same simple way as every project we do: 50% at signing, 25% when installation is scheduled, 25% on install day, with no interest.

Custom cabinets don't just fit the wall — they fit the way you cook, fold, work and put things away.
