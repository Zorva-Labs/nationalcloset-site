Home Depot & Lowe's Closet Installation vs. a Custom Closet Company

If you've searched "does Home Depot install closets" or "does Lowe's do closet installation," you deserve a straight answer. Big-box stores mostly sell DIY kits and stock components you assemble yourself, with installation sometimes available as a separate, subcontracted add-on. Here's an honest comparison with what a full-service custom company does differently — and why it often costs less than you'd think.

Does Home Depot or Lowe's actually install closets?

It's one of the most common questions we hear from folks around Nashville, Gallatin, Hendersonville, and Murfreesboro: "Does Home Depot install closets? Does Lowe's do closet installation?" The honest answer is that it depends on the store, the market, and what you're buying. Big-box home-improvement stores are terrific at a lot of things, but a fully custom closet built to your exact room is generally not one of them. Here's a fair, plain-English look at what those stores typically offer versus what a dedicated custom company does differently.

What the big-box stores generally sell

Walk into most home-improvement stores and the closet aisle is built around DIY kits you assemble yourself. You'll usually find two families of products: coated-wire systems (the metal shelving that mounts on wall tracks) and pre-cut laminate tower kits in fixed sizes. Both are designed to be carried out the door and put together with a drill on a Saturday. Offerings and brands vary quite a bit by store and by market, so what's stocked in one Middle Tennessee location may differ from the next.

Most chains also offer an online closet design tool. These are handy for rough planning, but they're limited to the manufacturer's stock components and standard dimensions. You pick from preset widths and configurations, and the tool snaps everything to what the kit can do — not to the real, out-of-square, sloped-ceiling reality of your actual closet.

Is installation available — and what does it cost?

Installation is sometimes offered, and this is where expectations and reality tend to part ways. When a big-box store installs a closet, the work is typically handled by a subcontracted third-party crew, and they're installing the stock kit you bought — not a system custom-built to your room. The labor is usually a separate line item on top of the kit price, and pricing varies widely by market, closet size, and whichever contractor the store assigns that week.

Because we won't invent numbers we can't verify, we'll say it plainly: a "cheap" kit can get noticeably less cheap once measuring, delivery, and installation labor are added — and those costs aren't always obvious until late in the process. If you want to understand what drives real pricing, we broke it down in what a custom closet actually costs.

The fit-and-quality gap

The bigger issue isn't even price — it's fit and durability. A stock kit is built to standard sizes, so in a real closet you often end up with filler strips, awkward gaps, or wasted space where the kit didn't quite match the wall. Coated-wire shelving sags under folded sweaters and lets small items tip through the gaps. Thin laminate panels and cam-lock hardware can loosen over years of daily use.

Depth matters too. Many stock and wire systems are built around a 12-inch shelf depth, which is fine for some items but tight for folded stacks, bins, and larger shoes. We build to a standard 14-inch depth so shelves hold more without crowding the hanging space — a small difference you feel every single day. Frequently asked questions

Does Home Depot install closets?
Sometimes, depending on the store and market. When installation is offered, it's typically handled by a subcontracted third-party crew installing a stock kit you purchased, with labor billed as a separate cost on top of the kit price. It varies by location, so check with your specific store.

How much does Home Depot charge to install a closet?
There's no single fixed price — it varies by market, closet size, kit chosen, and the contractor assigned. Labor is generally a separate line item added to the kit cost. Because those numbers move around, we recommend getting the full installed total in writing before you commit.

Does Lowe's do closet installation?
Like other big-box stores, Lowe's primarily sells DIY wire and laminate closet kits, and installation may be available in some markets through third-party installers. Offerings vary by store, so confirm locally what's included and what's billed separately.
