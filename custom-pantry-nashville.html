Custom Pantries · Nashville & Middle TN

Custom Pantries, Pantry Cabinets & Shelving — Built to the Inch

A great pantry is the hardest-working storage in the house. National Closet Company designs, builds and installs custom pantries across Nashville and Middle Tennessee — from adjustable pantry shelving and full pantry cabinets to butler and walk-in pantry organization — engineered to the inch so every can, box and small appliance has a home. Free in-home design, honest pricing.

From wire shelves to a custom pantry that fits everything

Most pantries start life as a few tiers of builder wire shelving — sagging, wasting the corners, and leaving dead space up high and down low. We replace it with a custom pantry designed around your food, your appliances and your habits: taller sections for cereal and small appliances, shorter runs for cans and jars, deep pantry cabinets for bulk, and continuous corner shelving that turns dead space into prime real estate.

Butler pantry, walk-in pantry or kitchen pantry — we build them all

Whatever kind of pantry you have, we build custom storage to fit it:

  • Walk-in pantry — floor-to-ceiling adjustable shelving and cabinets that make a dedicated pantry room hold far more, neatly.
  • Butler pantry — furniture-grade cabinetry and counter storage for the space between your kitchen and dining room, perfect for serving and staging.
  • Kitchen pantry — reach-in and cabinet pantries that squeeze maximum organized storage out of a small footprint.

Pantry cabinets & adjustable pantry shelving

The two building blocks of a great pantry are pantry cabinets and pantry shelving, and most of our custom pantries combine both. Open, fully adjustable shelving gives you flexible, see-everything storage that moves as your needs change. Cabinets with doors hide clutter for a clean, built-in, furniture-grade look. We build from durable 3/4-inch furniture board with soft-close hardware, so shelves stay flat and level under a full load of canned goods year after year — no sagging like wire.

A custom pantry doesn't just hold more — it makes everything easy to find, on shelves built for exactly what you store.

How much does a custom pantry cost?

Most custom pantries run about $1,500 to $5,000 installed, depending on size, whether you choose open pantry shelving or full pantry cabinets, and add-ons like pull-outs, drawers and lighting. You'll get an exact, no-obligation price at your free in-home design — never an estimate that changes later. See our full custom closet & pantry cost guide for real numbers.
