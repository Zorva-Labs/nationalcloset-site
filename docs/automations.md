# Mail, the cron and the morning sweeps

## Mail
- Every send goes over the Gmail API through the Google Workspace service account (`GOOGLE_SA_EMAIL`, `GOOGLE_SA_PRIVATE_KEY`; domain-wide delegation for gmail.send and gmail.readonly), acting as `GOOGLE_WORKSPACE_USER` (hello@ by default). The account's name and client id are in `site.json → mail`.
- Customer mail is from `MAIL_FROM` (National Closet Company <hello@nationalclosetco.com>). Staff alerts go to `STAFF_EMAIL` (hello@), sent as `ALERT_FROM_USER` (crm@, `alertSender`), so they are never self-addressed.
- **Website lead alerts** (`_lib/lead-alert.js`): `/api/contact` emails the team the moment a lead saves, listing only the fields the customer gave (the form asks for a name and a phone only — an alert that printed every empty field read like spam, 2026-10-01). When the customer adds an email, the space, an address or a note on the details step, `/api/contact-address` sends a second alert with everything known, in the same Gmail thread (Message-ID `<lead-<id>.alert@nationalclosetco.com>` on the first, In-Reply-To on the second), Reply-To the customer.
- `_lib/email.js` checks recipient domains over DoH and never sends spoof-shaped self-mail (a foreign Reply-To on a self-addressed message is dropped).
- A new lead gets the welcome mail from `_lib/lead-ack.js` once it has an email address (`docs/tracking.md`).
- Inbound: `/api/internal/email-sync` (`_lib/email-sync.js`) reads the Inbox and the Sent folder into `email_messages`, matched to contacts, leads and projects; mail the CRM composed carries `X-NCC-Origin: crm` and is skipped. Its cursor is the `email_sync_state` row: it needs its INBOX seed row (mailbox `INBOX`, `uid_validity` 2 marks a Gmail cursor).

## The cron
- Scheduling: Worker `nationalcloset-email-cron` (`~/nationalcloset-cron`, `*/3 * * * *`) POSTs `/api/internal/email-sync`, `/advance-jobs`, `/proposal-expiry` with `Authorization: Bearer $NCC_CRON_SECRET` (the Pages secret `CRON_SECRET`). Each endpoint also takes a CRM session, for a manual run.
- `/advance-jobs`, on every tick: a job whose install date has come moves from `scheduled_install` to `installing` (from 7am Central on the day, since 2026-10-17; a date already past moves at any hour), the client gets the stage email, and the balance invoice goes out (`docs/crm.md`). Then the customer's reminders, the install-eve reminder, the crew briefs and the review requests, each also runnable alone (`/api/internal/appointment-reminders`, `/api/internal/review-requests`).
- `/proposal-expiry`: the "expires tomorrow" reminder, and a lapsed proposal flips to expired.
- Central time: every sweep and the traffic page read the clock through the IANA zone in `_lib/dates.js`, never a flat offset, because Central is UTC-5 in daylight time and UTC-6 from November to March. `centralNow()` is the wall clock in the DB's naive Central form, `todayCentral()` the date, `centralAt(ms)` the Central clock at any instant, and `centralMidnightUtc(date)` the UTC instant a Central day starts, for rows stamped in UTC. Before 2026-09-25 the reminders, the review requests, the install-day flip and the traffic page's "today" used a flat UTC-5 (`CHANGELOG.md`).

## Day-before and morning-of notices (since 2026-10-17)
Every visit on the CRM calendar with a customer is announced twice, to the customer and to the crew: **the day before, 9am–8pm Central**, and **the morning of, from 7am Central** while the visit is still ahead. The hours are constants in `_lib/event-notices.js` (`EVE_FROM_HOUR`, `EVE_UNTIL_HOUR`, `MORNING_FROM_HOUR`); the clock is `centralNow()`.

| | Day before (9am–8pm) | Morning of (7am) |
|---|---|---|
| Appointment (consultation, measure, install, service): customer | reminder, "tomorrow at …" | reminder, "today at …" |
| Appointment: crew (assignees, else the whole roster) | consult brief, "Tomorrow …" | consult brief, "Today …" |
| Install date on a job (`projects.install_date`): customer | "Your install is tomorrow", clear the space | the `job_installing` stage email, sent when the job moves at 7am |

- **Once each, per start time:** every send is a row in `event_notices` (migration 0074) keyed on (entity, notice, the start time it was for). The sweep inserts the row before it sends and sends only if the insert took, so the 3-minute cron never sends twice. A rescheduled visit no longer matches its old rows and gets fresh reminders, with nothing to reset. `outcome` is `sent`, `failed`, `skipped:no_email` or `skipped:fresh_booking`. `appointments.reminder_sent_at` and `team_brief_sent_at` are superseded; 0074 copied their history in.
- **Fresh bookings:** a customer reminder is skipped (and recorded as skipped) when the appointment was created less than 6 hours earlier, or, for an install, when the "install scheduled" email went out less than 6 hours earlier: the confirmation has just gone. The crew brief is never skipped. A visit booked after 8pm for the next day gets the morning-of only; one that starts before 7am gets the day-before only.
- `_lib/appointment-reminders.js`: the customer's appointment reminders (`sweepConsultationReminders`, wording per type: the design visits keep the cancel link and the 45–60 minutes; install and service rows ask for a reply or a call instead) and the install-eve reminder (`sendInstallEveReminders`). Both log to `email_messages` (`consult_reminder` for the morning-of, `visit_reminder_eve`, `install_reminder_eve`) and the activity log.
- No crew brief for a job's install date yet: installs on jobs carry a date, not a time, and the brief's "WHEN" line needs one. Only appointment rows are briefed.

## The consult brief
- **Consult brief** (`_lib/consult-brief.js`): the day before and the morning of every appointment (consultation, measure, install, service; the windows above), every assigned team member is emailed the client's name, phone, email, the time, the address linked to Google Maps, rooms, notes, who else is going and a `/crm/calendar.html?appt=<id>` deep link. Sent as crm@ (`alertSender`), one email per assignee, **never to the client**, and deliberately not written to `email_messages` — that table is the client conversation; the record is an `activity_log` entry on the appointment. Nobody assigned → the whole active roster, and the email says so.
- Wording is the `consult_brief` row in `email_templates` with a byte-identical `FALLBACK` in the module (keep the two in step; migrations 0070 + 0071 are the source). Never write "Today" into it — the manual send can go out days early; use `{{day_word}}` / `{{day_relative}}` / `{{greeting}}`. A token the sender doesn't supply is blanked, never mailed as a literal.
- Guarded by its `event_notices` rows (`crew_eve`, `crew_morning`), claimed before sending (`centralNow()` uses the IANA zone, not a flat -5). Manual early send: `POST /api/appointments/[id] {action:"send_team_brief"}` ("Send crew brief" on the appointment), which claims no row, so the scheduled briefs still go.

## Review requests
- `_lib/review-requests.js`: completed projects 12 h+, design visits 2+ days (within 30, and not for a lead marked lost or a client with a signed job), 9am–8pm CT, once per contact per 90 days, ten per tick, email only — **no SMS, owner declined Twilio**. The wording is the `review_request` and `review_request_visit` rows in `email_templates`; the link in them is `/review` (`docs/content.md`).
