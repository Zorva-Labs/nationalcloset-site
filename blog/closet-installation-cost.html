How Much Does Closet Installation Cost?

If you're pricing out a new closet, "installation cost" is the number that trips people up most. Sometimes it's baked into the quote, sometimes it's a separate line, and sometimes it doesn't show up until the crew is standing in your bedroom. Here's what closet installation actually costs across Middle Tennessee, what drives the price up or down, and how National Closet Company folds installation into one honest, all-inclusive price.

The short answer on closet installation cost

For a professionally installed custom closet system, most Middle Tennessee homeowners land somewhere between $1,000 and $2,500 for a reach-in and $3,000 to $10,000+ for a walk-in — installed, not just the parts. Those ranges already include the labor to build it into your wall. The trouble starts when a company quotes you the materials and treats installation as a separate charge you discover later. That's where a "good deal" quietly turns into a bigger bill.

What actually drives the price

Closet cost isn't one flat number because no two closets are the same. A few factors move the needle more than anything else:

  • Size and linear footage. More wall space means more shelving, rods, and drawers — the single biggest driver of price.
  • Reach-in vs. walk-in. A walk-in has more surfaces to outfit on multiple walls, so it costs more than a single-wall reach-in. (More on that in reach-in vs. walk-in closets.)
  • Materials. Furniture-grade laminate and thermally-fused melamine hold up far better than the thin, wire-and-particleboard kits at the big-box store — and they're what we use as standard.
  • Complexity. Drawers, soft-close hardware, pull-out hampers, shoe fences, glass-front doors, and lighting all add cost because they add function.
  • Shelf depth. Our standard 14-inch shelves hold more than the 12-inch shelves many competitors install — a small spec with a big impact on usable storage. See 12-inch vs. 14-inch shelf depth.

For a fuller breakdown of the parts that make up a quote, our custom closet cost guide walks through it line by line.

Reach-in installation cost

A reach-in is your standard single-wall closet — one opening, one set of walls to outfit. Because there's less to build, it's the most affordable custom project, typically $1,000 to $2,500 installed. The install itself is quick: a straightforward reach-in usually goes in during a single visit. When you see a reach-in "kit" advertised for a few hundred dollars, remember that's the box on the shelf — assembly and mounting are on you, or on a handyman you hire separately.

Walk-in installation cost

A walk-in wraps storage around two, three, or four walls, often with a center island, seating, or a dressing area. That's more material, more layout, and more labor, so pricing runs $3,000 to $10,000 and up, installed. A larger walk-in with premium features climbs from there — you can see a real one we built in our $6,700 walk-in breakdown. Even at the top of the range, a standard closet is installed by our own crew in about a day.

Why box-store and DIY "installation" adds up

Here's the catch with the big-box route. The sticker price is for the components only. Installation is a separate service — often through a third-party contractor the store refers out — billed by the hour or as an add-on. By the time you total the kit, the delivery, the install labor, and the return trips for parts that didn't fit, the "cheap" option isn't cheap anymore. And you're coordinating multiple vendors, none of whom owns the final result.

DIY looks free until you count your weekend, the tools, the wall anchors that pull out of drywall, and the shelf that ends up an inch off level. For a small reach-in, DIY can work. For anything custom, the labor you're avoiding is exactly the labor that makes the closet last.

Frequently asked questions

Does closet installation cost extra with National Closet Company?
No. Installation is always included in your single all-inclusive price, alongside design, furniture-grade materials, delivery, and the warranty. You'll never get a separate labor bill after the fact — the number we quote is the number you pay.

How much does it cost to install a reach-in closet?
A professionally installed reach-in system in Middle Tennessee typically runs about $1,000 to $2,500, depending on size, materials, and features like drawers or soft-close hardware. That figure already includes the installation labor.

How much does a walk-in closet installation cost?
Walk-in closets generally range from about $3,000 to $10,000 or more, installed. The price depends on how many walls you outfit, the accessories you choose, and finishes like islands or lighting. A standard walk-in is installed by our crew in roughly one day.

Why is store-bought closet installation billed separately?
Big-box stores sell the components at the sticker price and treat installation as a separate service, often through a third-party contractor billed hourly or as an add-on. Once you total the kit, delivery, and labor, the real cost is usually much higher than it first appears.

How long does closet installation take?
A standard closet is installed in about one day by our own crew. Larger walk-ins with extensive features can take a bit longer, but most projects are completed in a single visit with minimal disruption to your home.
