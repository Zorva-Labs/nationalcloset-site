Why look for a Closets by Design alternative?

Closets by Design is a big national brand best known for its steady “40% off” and “free installation” promotions. The trouble with permanent-sale pricing is that the discount has to come from somewhere — it's baked into the starting number. Many homeowners come to us after a Closets by Design quote wanting to know what a closet actually costs without the promo gymnastics.

Our answer is simple: we quote one honest price, cut to the bone from the start. No 40%-off theater, because we never marked it up 40% to begin with. You deal directly with the small, local family that builds and installs your system — not a national sales operation.

The same custom quality — see it for yourself

We build from solid 3/4-inch furniture board — thick and rigid, so shelves stay flat under a full load — finished with soft-close hinges and timeless Shaker-style doors. The result looks and performs like fine cabinetry, engineered to the inch for your space.
