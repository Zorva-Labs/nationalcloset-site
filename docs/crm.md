# The CRM — `/crm`

`crm/` is the UI (30 static pages over `crm-app.js` and `crm-app.css`), `functions/api/` the API and `functions/_lib/` the shared code, over the D1 `nationalcloset-crm`. Mail and the scheduled sweeps are in `docs/automations.md`.

## Sign-in
- Logins (`admin_users`) sign in at `/crm/login.html` (styled by `admin.css`): the admin hello@nationalclosetco.com, password `NCC_CRM_PASSWORD` in `~/.env`; and Terry McCarthy, tmccarthy33@yahoo.com (since 2026-10-03, Michael; the password is Terry's and is not kept in `~/.env`). Both are admins. The same session opens `/crm/traffic` (`docs/traffic.md`); the traffic card on the dashboard is admin-only.
- **Roles (2026-10-21, Michael; migration 0077 `admin_users.role`):** `admin` sees and does everything. `sales` gets leads, clients, jobs, the pipeline, the calendar and scheduling, proposals, contracts, invoices and estimates (and reads the dashboard, search, history, the team, vendors and the templates to fill its pickers); not expenses and bills, vendors' edits, reports, traffic, the inbox, the team, template editing or the lead import. `installer` sees only the jobs they are assigned to (`/crm/my-jobs.html`, `/crm/my-job.html`, the install worksheet), read-only and with no money, and on the calendar only those jobs' installs and any visit they are on. An installer is linked to their jobs by email: the `team_members` row with the login's email, and `assignments` for the job; a login with no roster row sees no jobs.
- The server is the gate: `requireAuth` (`_lib/auth.js`) asks `canCall(role, method, path)` in `_lib/roles.js` before any CRM endpoint runs and answers 403, so **a new endpoint is admin-only until it is listed there**. The installer's per-job scoping is in `/api/crew/jobs` (no prices: the accepted option's line descriptions, rooms and quantities only), `GET /api/appointments` and `/api/appointments/:id`, and `/api/attachments/:id`. `crm-app.js` (`ROLE_PAGES`, `ROLE_HOME`) hides the nav a role can't use and redirects a page it can't open (an installer's `project.html?id=N` goes to `my-job.html?id=N`), but that is convenience, not security.
- **Managing sign-ins:** `/crm/team.html` → CRM access (admins only, through `/api/logins`): give a team member a login, change a role, reset a password (it signs them out) or remove a login. A new password is shown once and never stored in the clear. There is always one admin, and nobody changes or removes their own login. Logins whose email is not on the roster are listed under "Other sign-ins". The agency passwords sign in as login #1 (hello@, admin).
- `node crm/setup-admin.mjs <email> [pw] [--role admin|sales|installer]` sets a password (a new login defaults to sales; an existing one keeps its role unless `--role` is given): it hashes it locally (PBKDF2-SHA256), writes the hash to D1 through the Cloudflare API with `CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN`, and prints the password (it makes one up if none is given). It does not touch `~/.env`.

## The pipeline
- leads → contacts → projects → proposals → contracts → invoices (paid by check) → jobs advanced by the cron (`docs/automations.md`); the conversions between them are in `_lib/lifecycle.js`.
- Every sent proposal is valid for two days (`EXPIRY_DAYS` in `_lib/proposal-expiry.js`).
- **Hand-over on a tablet (2026-10-21, Michael):** the proposal's bottom bar has "Save & open for customer" beside Save and Save & Send while it is a draft ("Open for customer" once sent). It saves, then opens the customer's own page (`/proposal/?t=…`) in a new tab (opened before the save, because iPad Safari blocks a `window.open` after an await; it falls back to the same tab), so the customer picks an option, accepts and signs on the salesman's tablet. No email goes out. A draft is on no clock: the customer page and `/api/public/proposal` never call a draft expired, even one re-opened with an old `valid_until`.
- **Changing options after signing (2026-10-17):** a customer can switch to another option of the same proposal until money moves on the deal, even after signing (`_lib/option-change.js`). "Switch to this option" on the proposal page (after a confirm) voids the live contract and every unpaid invoice of the deal, then makes a new contract and deposit for the new option. The office gets an "Option changed after signing" alert. The deal is locked once any invoice of it is paid or processing or has an amount paid. Then the customer is told to call. Leaving Option 2 (`wallprep`) hands the job card's price back to the contracts, unless it was set by hand. The contract page (signed, unpaid) and the deposit's pay page link back to the options. A voided contract or invoice link points to its replacement.
- Contract types (`validTypes` in `_lib/lifecycle.js`): `custom_order`, `wallprep` (with wall repair and paint), `install_only` and `repair`. The live wording of `custom_order` and `wallprep` is in D1 `document_templates`, rows 1 and 6 (row 4 is the two-option proposal, row 5 the estimate's standard notes). `install_only` and `repair` have no row and use the code's fallbacks, which also stand in for any missing row: `FALLBACK_TERMS` for a contract made from an accepted proposal, and `TERMS_BY_TYPE` in `functions/api/contracts/index.js` for one made in the CRM directly.
- `_lib/lifecycle.js`: `PAYMENT_SCHEDULE` is the 50/25/25 terms (50% at signing, or more if the materials and shipping need it; 25% when the materials are in and the install is scheduled; 25% on install day; a pay-in-full proposal replaces it with one payment), and `FALLBACK_TERMS` carry the lifetime warranty (install-only and repair work carry 90 days).
- `QUOTE_MARKUP_RATE=0`: prices are entered with any markup already in them (the var is a fraction, clamped 0–25%, added to every proposal and estimate).

## Invoices and payment
- `_lib/invoices.js`: deposit, scheduling (25%), balance (the last 25%, billed on install day by the cron), full and custom invoices. **Invoice the full remaining balance, not 25%** (Michael, 2026-10-06): a checkbox beside the install date, offered while the job has none (the job's action card and the Schedule modal), sends `bill_balance: true` with the date. That PATCH then raises the balance invoice at once (`createInvoice`'s `early`: "Remaining balance (N%) — your installation is scheduled", and an email note that nothing else is due) in place of the scheduling one. The install-day hop dedups against it, so nothing more is billed, each emailed with a link to `/invoice/`. A paid deposit or full payment books the job.
- **A job can carry more than one deal** (a second proposal accepted after booking — Don Bruce, the shoe tower then the laundry room). The job's value is every signed contract once, a re-signed revision of the same proposal counted once (`_lib/job-total.js`, used by the jobs list, the job card, receivables and billing). Each contract gets its own deposit invoice (`existingInvoice` matches the deposit by contract or proposal); scheduling and balance bill what is left of the whole job. Accepting or signing never moves a booked job back to proposal stage.
- **Completing a job bills what is still owed** (`billOnCompletion`): the balance is raised if it never was; money still uninvoiced (a contract raised after the balance went out) becomes one more invoice for the remainder; and an unpaid balance invoice is emailed again, unless it went out in the last 12 hours (install day's own invoice, then "completed" that afternoon). A re-sent invoice asks for what is left on it after any partial payment.
- **A discount on the final payment** (Michael, 2026-10-06): once a job is booked (contracted → completed), the job's Invoices tab and its action card carry "Discount on final payment" (in $ or % of the job total, a reason the customer sees, and Remove). It is stored on the job (`projects.final_discount_cents`, `final_discount_note`), not as an invoice line. Storing it as a line would let `billOnCompletion` bill it back as a "remainder". `getProjectBilling` takes it off the job total, so the final invoice, the completion sweep, Receivables and the jobs list all count the lower figure. The scheduling payment is figured as if there were no discount, so all of it lands on the final invoice. `POST /api/projects/:id/discount` also moves money already billed: a bigger discount comes out of what is not invoiced yet first, then off open invoices (the balance one first). A smaller one puts the money back. An invoice brought to $0 is voided; one brought down to what was already paid is settled. It refuses more than is still owed, because collected money needs a refund instead. `invoices.discount_cents` is the part an invoice carries: the CRM sheet, the email and `/invoice/` show it as a discount line. Nothing is emailed when it is set.
- **Paid by check (2026-10-21, Michael: "remove the connection to stripe and just set invoices to accept a check"):** `/invoice/?t=…` shows what is due (the deposit / pay-in-full choice stays) and "Make your check payable to James Blair", handed to the Project Manager; the invoice email, the receipt for a part payment, the CRM invoice's footer and the contract terms say the same. The office records the check with Record payment (Check, Cash or Other, with the check number as the reference), which is what marks an invoice paid and books the job. `_lib/stripe.js` and `/api/stripe/webhook` are deleted, the Pages secrets `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY`/`STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET` and the `STRIPE_PUBLISHABLE_KEY` var removed, Stripe's hosts are out of the CSP, and both webhook endpoints (live and sandbox) are disabled, not deleted. The 28 card payments before then keep their `paid_method`, `stripe_payment_intent_id` and `fee_cents`. The deposit is half the total (see below). Never offer Klarna, Affirm or any buy-now-pay-later method (Michael).

## Getting around
- **Breadcrumbs:** `SSCrm.mount` puts a hidden `nav#page-crumbs` above every page title. `SSCrm.setCrumbs(items)` fills it. `SSCrm.jobCrumbs(projectId, name, tab, current)` writes Jobs › job › tab › document and turns the page's `#back-link` into "← Back to job" (`/crm/project.html?id=…#tab=…`). The proposal, contract, invoice and estimate pages call it; a document with no job gets its list instead (Invoices › INV-…). The job page shows Jobs › job, a lead that has a job shows Jobs › job › Lead, and the install worksheet (its own print page, not mounted) shows Jobs › job › Install worksheet, hidden in print. Messages open inside the job's Messages tab.
- **Visits open their job (2026-10-17):** an appointment rarely carries `project_id`; the booking carries the lead, and the job hangs off the lead. `/api/dashboard` (`upcoming`) and `GET /api/appointments/:id` return `job_id` = `project_id`, else the newest job with that `lead_id`. A dashboard upcoming row opens the job, else the lead, else the calendar; the calendar's event card has "Open job →" (or "Open lead →"). The calendar's deep link is `?appt=N`; the old `?focus=` links opened nothing.
- **No cost, labor or profit math anywhere** (2026-10-21, Michael: "remove all calculations for profit, labor etc...", for everyone): the job card's "Expenses & profit" card, the Jobs list's Net profit and Labor columns and profit tiles, the proposals list's Profit column, the Reports P&L view (its API `functions/api/reports/index.js`), payables' estimated installer labor, cash flow's modeled job costs and the cost lines of `calc.html` are all gone, with `functions/api/projects/[id]/financials.js`, `resolveFinancials` and the cost formula in `_lib/financials.js`. `job_financials` and `proposals.cost_rates` stay in the D1 with what was entered, and nothing reads or writes them. Bills logged under Expenses are real payables and still show in Payables and Cash flow.
- **The deposit is 50% of what the customer pays** (2026-10-21, Michael: "Always 50%"), with no materials floor: `depositForTotal(total)` in `_lib/financials.js`, mirrored by `depositFor` on `/proposal/` and by `calc.html`. A deposit typed on the contract still wins. The contract templates lost "or, if greater, the amount required to cover the materials and shipping" (migration 0078); contracts sent before keep their terms.

## The UI on a phone
- **Mobile (`crm-app.css`, the `≤700px` block at the end).** Two rules that bit hard: a data table lives in `.tbl`, which must keep `overflow-x: auto` — `overflow: hidden` there clips the table with no way to scroll; and a grid track that holds free content must be `minmax(0, 1fr)`, never a bare `1fr`, which will not shrink below min-content. A page that sets `grid-template-columns` in an inline `style=` beats the stylesheet's media query, so those need `!important` or a class (`.tpl-layout`, `.lead-grid`). No CRM page may have content off-screen and out of reach at 375px; re-measure after adding a page. A list whose last cell holds buttons (the job's invoices, `.inv-tbl`) becomes cards at ≤700px rather than a sideways-scrolled row: the pinned first cell otherwise covers them.

## Contrast
- **Accent colors split by job, and text is measured** (the terracotta `:root` block at the end of `crm-app.css`, over the file's first, blue one). A color that fills well is too light to write with: the brand terracotta `--accent` (#D2683F) carries white at 3.63:1 and reads as text at 3.44:1. So `--accent` is fills/borders/focus rings only, `--accent-fill` (+`-hover`) is a filled control with a white label, and `--accent-text` (+`-hover`) is the accent as text — it clears 4.5:1 on every surface the CRM paints on. `--accent-ink` and `--accent-hover` are aliases into that scale so inline `style=` on the pages resolves correctly. The `--s-*` tokens are pill text only and are already taken down to ≥4.7:1. `admin.css` carries the same split for the login page as `--color-brass-fill` / `--color-brass-text`.
- Nothing in the CRM is below AA as painted at 1280px and 375px: re-measure after adding a color, and never put text on `--accent` itself.

## Mail templates and times
- **Templates → Email edits the plain text AND the HTML.** Every automated send prefers `body_html`; a template edited only in `body_text` changes nothing in the recipient's inbox. The editor exposes the HTML box for any template that has one.
- `reminder_sent_at` / `team_brief_sent_at` are stamped with `datetime('now')`, which is **UTC** — unlike `start_at` and the other appointment datetimes, which are naive Central wall-clock. Anything that displays them has to say so (`fmtStampUtc` in calendar.html).

- **Book the painter (2026-10-17):** a job with a live Option 2 (`wallprep`) contract the customer has signed needs the painter booked (`_lib/painter.js`: `wallPaintSql`, `painterNeededSql`; the APIs return `wall_paint`). A customer who switches back to Option 1 voids the Option 2 contract, so the flag goes too.
- **Wall finishing (`wallprep`):** until 2026-10-21 accepting a wallprep proposal wrote Option 2's closet and wall onto `job_financials` for the profit math; it writes nothing there now. The job's value is its contracts like any other job.
