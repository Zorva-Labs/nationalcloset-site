Every visit by a real person, counted by the site itself.

The site counts each page a visitor actually opens, with its own first-party beacon on its own address — nothing for an ad blocker to recognize, and no crawler or script, because only a real browser sends it. The forms are the ones saved in the CRM. This is the number to quote.

Why these numbers differ from the Edge tab.
  • Search Console covers Google search only — not Bing, not people typing the address, not ads.
  • Google withholds rare searches to protect privacy, so the clicks here add up to less than the visits Google really sent.
  • It runs two to three days behind and arrives weekly. A quiet first few weeks on a new site is normal, not broken.

Sessions by device

Phone, computer or tablet, as Google saw it.

It will read lower than the Edge tab, and that is not an error. A script in the browser never sees a visit where:
  • an ad or tracking blocker stopped it loading — routinely a fifth to a third of visitors;
  • a privacy browser or a network-level blocker stripped it before it ran;
  • the person left before the page finished loading;
  • Google judged the visit automated and filtered it out, which occasionally catches real people.
The Edge tab is written before any script runs at all, which is why both are here.
