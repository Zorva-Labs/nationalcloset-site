Family-owned · Gallatin, Tennessee

Family-owned. Not a franchise.

National Closet Company is a small Middle Tennessee family business that designs, builds and installs custom closets at a price normal families can afford. Here is who you are dealing with, and why we build the way we do.

Hi, I'm Michael Blair. My family started National Closet Company in Gallatin because we kept watching neighbors pay national-brand prices for closets that were marked up just so they could be "discounted."

We do it differently. Every project gets a free in-home design you can see in 3D, one honest installed price, and two quotes so you can decide how much of the prep you want to handle yourself. Our shelves are 14 inches deep as standard, because the 12-inch shelves the big brands install don't fit real hangers and real bins.

We are a small team, so the person who measures your closet is the person who answers the phone, and most installs are done in a single day. We're happily obsessed with every shelf, drawer and pull-out. Around here they call us the closet freaks, and we're fine with that.

Michael BlairFounder, National Closet Company

What we stand behind

Our promise

  • One honest price. No "40% off" games on a marked-up list. The number your designer quotes is the number you pay.
  • Two quotes, always. Prep the space yourself for the lowest price, or go fully turnkey and we remove, patch, paint and install.
  • Deeper shelves. 14″ standard where the national brands install 12″, so hangers and bins actually fit.
  • Our own installers. No subcontractors. Most closets in a single day, spotless cleanup.
